# The Packet-Packing Worksheet

Use this when a portal (or a clerk, or an email thread) asks you to upload **several documents as one PDF packet**. Print one page, fill it in pencil-side, merge with confidence. Designed to be done in one sitting; nothing on this sheet requires software you do not already have.

- Every row filled? (A row without a purpose is a fax ghost; cut it or name why it travels.)
- Same-named duplicates dropped? (Insurance card appearing in two uploads, etc.)

## Part 2: Pre-merge checks (5 minutes)

- [ ] Every part passes the **60-second inspection** (chapter 08): real pages, right page counts, no blank backs, no swapped pages.
- [ ] Every part has been **renamed honestly** (date and content, starting with a number for order).
- [ ] Every scanned part that a human or AI will need to search later has **OCR applied** if it is image-only (chapter 07). Born-digital parts need nothing here.
- [ ] **Signatures verified** on every page that expects them: signed, dated, visible, on the right lines (chapter 04). Sign *before* merging into the packet.
- [ ] **Forms flattened** if they were interactive and the fields-won't-save gotcha applies (chapter 02).
- [ ] Decide: does the **portal size cap** (chapter 03) need *pre*-merger compression? If any single part is over the cap, compress *that part first*, not the merged packet.

## Part 3: Merge (the calm ritual, in one place)

1. **Rotate the odd-page-orientation parts first** so the packet reads upright throughout (PDF Arranger, Preview, or re-export).
2. **Merge in the numbered order from Part 1** into one file: `packet-final.pdf` (qpdf, PDF Arranger, or Preview — chapter 05).
3. **Name the merge with a date and a purpose**: `2026-11-15-home-loan-packet.pdf`.

## Part 4: Post-merge inspection (2 minutes, non-negotiable)

- [ ] Page order matches the manifest exactly (Part 1, left to right).
- [ ] Page-through at reading speed; **orientation**, dark scans, and sideways text all caught here.
- [ ] **File size** is under the portal cap with room to spare.
- [ ] Signatures are *still visible* after compression (if you compressed; a squash can blur faint ink).
- [ ] The copy you are about to send is **your dated copy** — one kept on your side even if the portal keeps its own.

## Part 5: After sending (30 seconds)

- [ ] **Portal receipt / confirmation number saved** to your notes or files.
- [ ] **Dated copy of the sent packet retained** (year-month-day plus purpose, sorted where you'll find it again).
- [ ] Working files (unmerged parts, compressed intermediates) archived or binned — your folder, spouse's eyebrows, and future self all benefit.

> One-page peace of mind. Fill it in during the coffee, merge it that same night.
