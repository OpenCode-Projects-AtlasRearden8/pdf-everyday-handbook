# Read Me First

Hello, friend. This is a handbook for *living* with PDFs — the awkward little files that show up in job applications, rental agreements, tax packets, medical portals, and every other corner of life where somebody wants "the form, signed, under 2 MB."

We wrote this the way we write everything at Asdale Dynamics: in plain English, step by step, never condescending. No jargon without a translation. No "just use this expensive tool." No shame if you have ever printed, signed, scanned, and THEN discovered the third page was blank. We have all been there.

## Who this is for

- Anyone who has ever been asked to "please upload a PDF" and felt their stomach drop.
- People who refuse to pay a monthly subscription just to fill in a form.
- Nurses, teachers, small-business owners, and authors who do their life admin in the margins of a busy day (low energy is expected here — everything is written to be done in one sitting, often in under ten minutes).
- Anyone who wants a local AI to read their paperwork *without* that paperwork leaving their house.

## What you will learn, chapter by chapter

- **What a PDF really is** — the "print job on paper" idea that explains almost every PDF mystery.
- **Fill in any form** — flat forms, interactive forms, and what to do when the field boxes refuse to appear.
- **Shrink a PDF** — get under an upload limit without making the text unreadable.
- **Sign it** — print-sign-scan versus careful digital signing, and when each is honest and safe.
- **Merge and split** — build one tidy packet, in the right order, under the size cap.
- **Pull text out** — copy-paste, extraction tools, and feeding text to a local AI safely.
- **OCR in plain English** — how a computer reads a photograph of text, and how to do it free, offline.
- **Is this PDF what it claims to be?** — a short, calm inspection routine before you trust or send a file.

Then three reference chapters you can jump straight to:

- **The packet-packing worksheet** — a print-and-pencil checklist for building a multi-document packet correctly on the first try.
- **Gotchas: learn from my scars** — a table of the traps, what each one does, and the fix.
- **Glossary** — every term in this handbook translated into human.

## How to use this handbook

1. Skim chapter 01 once. It is ten minutes of idea that pays off in every later chapter.
2. Use the middle chapters as *recipes*: open when the task appears, do the steps, close the book.
3. Keep the worksheet (chapter 09) next to you any time a portal asks for several documents in one upload.
4. When a PDF misbehaves, check the gotcha table (chapter 10) first — there is a good chance your exact scar is already listed.

## A note on privacy

This handbook is **local-first**. Wherever a task can be done with tools on your own computer, we show that path first, and we say so plainly. We also tell you exactly when a task tempts you to upload your document to a stranger's server — and what you are trading away when you do it. You decide; we just make the trade visible.
